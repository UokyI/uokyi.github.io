Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Plum Blossom Divination ("we", "our", or "this extension", known in Chinese as 梅花·易数) is a browser extension for traditional Chinese culture study and entertainment. It provides character-based Plum Blossom divination (梅花易数) when you select Chinese text on a webpage and choose the extension from the right-click menu. This privacy policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect any personal data.

This extension operates entirely and only on your local device. No data is ever transmitted, uploaded, or shared with us or with any external server. It has no analytics, no advertising, no subscriptions, and no network requests.

How the Extension Works

All divination is computed locally in your browser using local stroke-count data. When you select Chinese text and choose "Plum Blossom reading" from the right-click menu, the extension shows a single result card (hexagram, moving line, and body/use five-element fortune) on the current page. It never reads, modifies, or tracks the content of the pages you visit, and it takes no action except when you deliberately trigger it.

Data Storage

The extension uses Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) exclusively to save a single daily-use counter (the current date and how many readings were used that day), in order to enforce its once-per-day limit as part of its single purpose.

  • Current date
  • Number of readings used that day

This counter stays strictly on your local device and is never uploaded or shared. It contains no personal content and resets automatically each day. No other data is stored.

Permissions

  • contextMenus - Used solely to register the "Plum Blossom reading" item in the browser's right-click menu.
  • storage - Used solely to save the local daily-use counter described above.
  • scripting - Used solely to display the local result card on the page you are currently viewing.
  • activeTab - Grants temporary access to the active tab only when you deliberately trigger the extension; no broad host permissions are requested.

The extension requests no host_permissions, so it cannot access or read web content in the background.

Data Transmission

This extension does not transmit any data to any server. There is no cloud service, no telemetry, no tracking, and no third-party communication of any kind.

Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
