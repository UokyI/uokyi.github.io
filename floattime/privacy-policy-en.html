Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Effective date: August 30, 2026

1. Overview

This policy applies to the floatTime (Floating Countdown) app and its official website. We respect and protect your privacy. floatTime is a fully offline local tool: it collects no personal information, never connects to the internet, and never uploads or stores any data on a server.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. No account registration, phone number, or email is required to use this app.

3. Data Storage and Use

  • Settings such as your countdown task names, durations, floating bubble appearance colors and sizes are stored only in a private directory on your device (getFilesDir()), used solely for the app to function normally.
  • Usage history is likewise stored only locally on your device.
  • The data above is never transmitted over the network and cannot be read or collected by any third party. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

4. Permissions

To provide the floating countdown feature, the app requests the following permissions, each used only for its corresponding core function:

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): to show a draggable floating bubble on your screen.
  • Foreground service permission: to keep the countdown running in the background and display the floating bubble.
  • Notification permission: to display the persistent service notification.
  • Vibration permission (VIBRATE): to vibrate when the countdown finishes.

5. Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any ad SDKs, analytics SDKs, push SDKs, or third-party libraries, and does not share or trade any data with third parties.

6. In-App Privacy Policy Link

You can also view the online version of this policy from the App's "About" dialog or the "Privacy Policy" entry in the drawer menu: https://uokyi.github.io/floattime/privacy-policy-en.html

7. Children's Privacy

This app performs no data collection and has no account system, making it suitable for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date at the top. Material changes will be announced through app version release notes and similar channels.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us on GitHub: github.com/UokyI/floatTime
